Welcome to your New Evidence-Based Teaching and Learning Coach.

The NEB TLC is a teaching triage tool for 24/7 faculty guidance on acute pedagogical challenges. The recommendations are evidence-based instructional strategies for post-secondary courses reflecting culturally responsive, inclusive pedagogy and UDL best practices to accommodate learner variability.
